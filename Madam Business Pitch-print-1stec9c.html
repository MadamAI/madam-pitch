09 / 11 · why now
why now

A market hiding in plain sight.

The broadcast era is ending. Audiences — led by Black women — are moving to depth: smaller, identity-first platforms where creators, community and commerce share one space.

1 in 3

Gen Z deleted a broadcast social app in the past year. The open feed is fragmenting — and the migration is toward depth: smaller, truer spaces.

Axios, 2025
48%
