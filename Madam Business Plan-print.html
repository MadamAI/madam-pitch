04 / 10 · the jar
what we build · 01

The Jar.

A Jar is a living space around a passion — not a folder, not a board. She fills it with looks, products, links, notes and routines, and people connect through what's inside.

Around

A passion, a routine, a goal, an aesthetic — anything she cares about.

Inside

Posts, products, photos, notes, and the people she's learning from.

Shared

Public Jars let other women follow, save and recreate.

Hers

Private Jars are sacred. A space to hold who she's becoming.

A Jar can hold anything.

08 / 10 · business model
how we make money

Three revenue lines. One platform.

01 · Today
$$
Affiliate.

Every product saved into a Jar is shoppable. We take a cut on every checkout — creator earns, Madam earns.

Live at launch · 8–18% take
02 · This year
$$$
Jar subscriptions.

Creators charge a monthly fee for premium Jars — exclusive routines, members-only Journeys, locked product lists. Madam takes 15%.

Recurring · highest LTV line
03 · Future
$$$$
Ads.

Brands pay to appear inside relevant Jars and Daia answers — first-party signal, not third-party tracking. Unlocks at scale.

Phase 3 · sponsored placement
